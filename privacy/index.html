DevKartz Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

DevKartz is a Chrome extension that helps you save and organize developer websites. This policy explains what data the extension and this website handle. The short version: the extension keeps everything on your device.

The DevKartz extension

What it stores

The resources you add (names, URLs, descriptions, categories, tags and favorites) and your sort preference are saved in your browser using chrome.storage.local. This data stays on your device.

What it does not do

  • It does not create accounts or require sign-in.
  • It does not send your data to any server, including ours.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, trackers, or third-party services.
  • It does not sell or share your data.
  • It does not read your browsing history or page content.

Permissions

  • storage: saves your library and preferences locally.
  • activeTab: reads the title and address of the current tab only when you click “Save this tab”.
  • favicon: shows each saved site’s icon using the icon images Chrome already keeps in its own cache. DevKartz itself makes no network requests for icons and sends nothing anywhere. Sites Chrome has no icon for show a letter instead.
  • bookmarks (optional): requested only if you choose “Import Chrome bookmarks”. DevKartz reads your bookmark folders once so you can pick which to import, keeps only the folders you select as links in your local library, sends nothing anywhere, and releases the permission right after reading.

Your control

You can edit or delete any resource, export your library as a JSON file, or remove all data by uninstalling the extension. Exported files are saved where you choose and are not uploaded anywhere.

This website

This website is a static site. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and it does not collect information through forms. Like any website, our hosting provider (Vercel) may process technical data such as your IP address in routine server logs in order to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. The interactive demo on the home page runs entirely in your browser and saves nothing.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply to you.

Future changes

If DevKartz adds optional features such as paid plans or cross-device sync, this policy will be updated before those features are released, and any data they handle will be described here.
