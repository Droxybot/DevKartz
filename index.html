What’s inside

Small on purpose.

One job, done well. A fast shelf for the links developers reuse, with nothing to sign up for and nothing to configure.

  1. 01

    Save in one click

    Hit “Save this tab” and the title and address are captured. Add a note, a category and tags if you want to.

  2. 02

    Categories and tags

    One category per link, as many tags as you like. Click a tag in your list to filter by it.

  3. 03

    Search that keeps up

    Names, addresses, descriptions and tags are searched as you type. Press / to jump to the box.

  4. 04

    Favorites and sorting

    Star what you use daily. Sort by favorites, A–Z or newest. Copy a link, edit it, or undo a delete.

  5. 05

    Your data, portable

    Export your library as a JSON file, import one, or bring in the Chrome bookmark folders you choose. There’s also an optional starter set of twelve sites.

  6. 06

    Local by design

    Everything is kept in your browser’s own storage. No account, no server, no analytics.

How it works

Three moves. That’s the whole thing.

1

Save the page you’re on

Found a doc worth keeping? Click “Save this tab”. DevKartz grabs the title and address for you.

2

Give it a home

Pick a category and add a few tags, or skip it for now. You can edit anything later.

3

Find it in a second

Start typing. The list narrows as you go, so you land on the page instead of rebuilding the search.

Your links stay in your browser’s local storage. The required permissions are storage, activeTab and favicon (for site icons). A fourth, bookmarks, is asked for only if you import bookmarks, and is released right after.
